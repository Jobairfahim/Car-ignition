How It Works

How Ignition Systems Work

Inside the ignition cylinder are small components that must align precisely with the key. Over time, both the key and ignition wear down, causing alignment issues. Even small changes in alignment can create resistance or prevent the ignition from turning.

Common Signs of Ignition Problems

  • Key feels rough when inserted
  • Turning requires more effort than usual
  • Ignition responds inconsistently
  • Key only works after multiple attempts
  • Steering lock feels harder to release

These are early warning signs that should not be ignored. Fixing the issue early keeps the repair simple and more affordable.

⛔ Before It Gets Worse

Forcing the key when it does not turn smoothly is one of the most common causes of serious damage. What can happen:

  • Broken key inside the ignition
  • Internal component damage
  • Increased repair costs significantly
  • Complete ignition failure

Accurate Diagnosis Matters

Many starting issues are incorrectly blamed on the battery or starter. In reality, ignition wear is often the cause. Common causes include:

  • • Worn or damaged key
  • • Internal ignition wear
  • • Dirt or debris buildup
  • • Mechanical failure
  • • Damage from forcing the key

Repair vs Replacement

Not Every Ignition Needs Replacing

In many cases, the ignition can be repaired. Replacement is only necessary when the damage is truly beyond repair.

✅ When We Repair

  • Restoring internal alignment of worn components
  • Cleaning debris or buildup from the cylinder
  • Adjusting worn parts to restore smooth operation
  • Fixing intermittent response issues early
  • Extracting broken keys and recutting new ones

⚠️ When Replacement Is Needed

  • Damage is extensive and beyond repair
  • Forcing caused complete internal failure
  • Cylinder is cracked or structurally damaged
  • Repair is no longer cost-effective

Common Mistakes to Avoid

These mistakes often turn a simple repair into a bigger, more expensive problem.

Push-to-Start Systems

Push-to-Start Not Responding?

If your push-to-start system is not responding, try these steps first. If the issue continues, professional diagnosis is needed.

1

Hold the Key Fob Close

Hold the key fob directly next to the start button—low battery can affect range.

2

Use the Emergency Slot

If available on your vehicle, use the emergency key fob slot near the start button.

3

Check the Key Fob Battery

A weak or dead fob battery is a common cause. Try replacing it first.
